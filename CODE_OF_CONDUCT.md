# Code of Conduct

Be kind, be constructive, assume good intent. We want this to be a friendly place to build free software.

- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Critique the code and the idea, not the person.
- Don't submit work you don't have the right to share (copied code, logos, art or copy from other products).

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer, [@tommy-muckstack](https://github.com/tommy-muckstack).

This is a short stub. We intend to adopt the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in full.
