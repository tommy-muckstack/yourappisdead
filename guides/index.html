Guides

Practical, plain-language guides for anyone trying to spend less on software without making their work harder.

Paying for a dozen subscriptions is easy to drift into and surprisingly hard to untangle. These guides cover the parts that matter: working out what you actually pay for and use, finding free and open-source tools that do the same job, and deciding honestly when a paid product is still worth it. They also explain how to judge whether an open-source project is safe to rely on, what it takes to run one yourself, and how the apps on this site are built, including their limits.

Each guide is written to be useful on its own, whether or not you ever use an IsDead app. Where we refer to our own projects, we say what they do well and where they fall short. Start with the subscription audit if you want quick savings, or with the open-source checklist if you are about to move real work to a new tool.

  • How to audit your SaaS subscriptions

    Find every recurring charge, cut unused seats, and do the annual-versus-monthly math.

  • Free alternatives to the SaaS tools most teams pay for

    Category by category: what free tools cover, what you give up, and when to keep paying.

  • How to judge an open-source app before you rely on it

    Licenses, project activity, security, data export, hosting and support, explained plainly.

  • How to self-host an open-source SaaS app

    Environment variables, databases, sign-in, email, backups, updates, domains and costs.

  • How each IsDead app is built

    The rules, shared stack and storage choices behind every app, and how to contribute.
