Free Trello alternative: TrelloIsDead

Last reviewed October 26, 2026 · License: Apache-2.0 · Source on GitHub

TrelloIsDead is a free, open-source kanban app with boards, lists and cards, shared Workspaces, several board views, five built-in Power-Ups and rule-based automation. Boards are saved on the server, so teammates can work on the same board. It can't import from Trello, and it doesn't send email.

What Trello does, and what TrelloIsDead covers

Trello organizes work as boards made of lists, with cards that move from list to list as work progresses. Cards hold members, labels, dates, checklists, attachments and comments. Paid plans add more views (table, calendar, timeline, dashboard, map), more automation, admin controls and integrations with other tools through Power-Ups.

TrelloIsDead rebuilds most of that board experience. You drag cards between lists and drag lists by their headers. The card back has members, named labels, start and due dates with reminders, several checklists per card (items can have their own due date and assignee), attachments up to 4 MB or as links, covers, a location on an OpenStreetMap map, custom fields, a Markdown description, and comments with @mentions. The board can switch between Board, Table, Calendar, Timeline, Dashboard and Map views, all honoring the same filter.

It also handles the team side. You can create several Workspaces with admins and members, give board roles (admin, member, observer), and invite people by email address or by link. Edits are saved on the server as you make them, and other people's changes show up within a few seconds. Notifications cover assignments, mentions, watched cards and due reminders. Built-in Power-Ups (Voting, Card Aging, List Limits, Custom Fields, Card Numbers) and an automation builder with rules, card buttons and board buttons round it out. There are also 12 starter templates and a global search with Trello-style operators such as @me, #label and due:week.

What it costs

As of October 2026, Trello's pricing page lists four plans. Free costs nothing and allows up to 10 collaborators and 10 boards per Workspace, 250 automation command runs a month and 10 MB files. Standard is $5 per user per month billed annually ($6 billed monthly), Premium is $10 billed annually ($12.50 monthly), and Enterprise is $17.50 per user per month billed annually. Views beyond the basic board, such as calendar and timeline, are part of the paid tiers.

TrelloIsDead is free at yourappisdead.com/trelloisdead, with every feature available to every account. You can even use it without an account as a guest; signing in later moves your boards into your account. Running your own copy costs whatever your hosting and Firestore usage cost, or just a server with a disk if you use the built-in file storage.

How to switch from Trello

  1. Export each Trello board as a backup first: board menu, then Print, export and share, then JSON. Keep the files; TrelloIsDead can't read them yet, but they preserve your history.
  2. Create an account on TrelloIsDead (or start as a guest) and create a Workspace for your team.
  3. Create a board for each Trello board, from scratch or from one of the 12 templates, and add your lists.
  4. Move over the cards that are still open. For long lists, paste titles one at a time in the card composer; Enter adds a card and keeps the composer open.
  5. Re-create labels, custom fields and automation rules, then invite teammates by email or share an invite link. No email is sent, so send the link yourself.
  6. Run both tools side by side for a short while, then archive the old boards.

Running it yourself

TrelloIsDead is a Next.js 15 and React 19 app in TypeScript. Every board edit is an action that the browser applies immediately and the server re-checks and applies inside a transaction, so simultaneous edits don't overwrite each other. Storage is Firestore when FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON is set, and JSON files under .data/ otherwise. Locally, run npm ci, then NODE_OPTIONS=--unhandled-rejections=warn npm run dev and open http://localhost:3000/trelloisdead; guest and demo accounts work without Firebase. For production you also need AUTH_SECRET for guest sessions and the NEXT_PUBLIC_FIREBASE_* variables for sign-in.

Limitations

  • No outgoing email: invitations and notifications appear only inside the app. Password-reset emails still come from Firebase.
  • Collaborators' changes arrive by polling every 4 seconds rather than instantly.
  • Uploads are capped at 4 MB, and uploaded files aren't cleaned up when a board is deleted.
  • Drag and drop works with a mouse only; on touch screens you move cards with the Move action.
  • No importer for Trello boards, no third-party Power-Ups, and no Inbox, Planner, stickers or card mirroring.

FAQ

Can I import my Trello boards?

Not yet. The README lists a Trello importer as not built. Export your boards from Trello as JSON for safekeeping and recreate the active ones.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start as a guest. Sharing and invitations need an account, and when you sign in your guest boards move into it.

Can my team work on the same board?

Yes. Boards are stored on the server, Workspaces have members and roles, and changes reach other people within a few seconds.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. The board menu exports a board as JSON or CSV.

Is there a mobile app?

No native app. The web app was tested at phone width, but dragging cards on touch screens isn't supported.
