Free Linktree alternative: LinktreeIsDead

Last reviewed October 26, 2026 · License: Apache-2.0 · Source on GitHub

LinktreeIsDead is a free, open-source link-in-bio page builder. You get one public page with your links, photo, bio and social icons, styled with themes, plus click and visitor insights. Features that Linktree keeps for paid plans, such as hiding the footer, scheduling links and longer analytics ranges, are available to everyone.

What Linktree does, and what LinktreeIsDead covers

Most social apps give you a single spot for a link in your profile. Linktree turns that spot into a small landing page listing everything you want people to find: your shop, newsletter, latest video, booking page and so on. On top of the free page, Linktree's paid plans add design control, deeper analytics, email collection tools, selling features and social media scheduling.

LinktreeIsDead covers the page itself and most of the tools around it. In the editor you paste a link and the app detects what it is, such as a video, a music track or a social profile. Beyond plain links, you can add headers, text, collections shown as a stack, grid or carousel, galleries, maps, FAQs, events, contact cards, discount codes, PDFs, RSS feeds and embeds from services like Calendly, Cal.com, Typeform, Google Forms and Canva. Individual links can be featured, scheduled to start and end, locked behind a code or age check, set to redirect, animated, or archived with their stats kept.

For design there are 20 themes, 37 fonts, wallpapers (solid, gradient, pattern, image or short video), button styles, colors, stickers, five header layouts, 27 social icons and an option to hide the footer. A live phone preview updates as you edit. Your page is served at /linktreeisdead/u/<handle>, and every link click passes through a counter that only redirects to the address you saved.

Insights cover 7, 28, 90 or 365 days, with views, clicks, unique visitors, traffic sources, locations, devices and per-link stats. Other sections include an audience list fed by your page's forms (with CSV export), a messaging inbox, a media kit, a digital business card with a QR code, a link shortener and an offline social planner. You can start as a guest without signing up; the page moves to your account when you sign in.

What it costs

Linktree has a free plan with unlimited basic links, plus Starter, Pro and Premium plans and custom Agency or Enterprise pricing. As of October 2026, the prices recorded in the project's price research are $8, $15 and $35 a month on monthly billing, or $6, $12 and $30 a month billed annually. The official pricing page loads its prices dynamically, so check there for your region and currency.

The hosted LinktreeIsDead costs nothing. Self-hosting means paying for your own hosting and Firebase usage; uploaded images and videos count against a 150 MB allowance per account.

How to switch from Linktree

  1. Open the app. You can work as a guest straight away, but sign in if you want to keep the page across devices.
  2. Choose your handle on the welcome screen. Your address will be yourappisdead.com/linktreeisdead/u/<handle>.
  3. Copy each link from your Linktree admin and paste it into Add. There's no importer, so this is done link by link.
  4. In Header, upload your photo, write your bio and switch on your social icons.
  5. Pick a theme and adjust buttons, fonts and colors under Design while watching the phone preview.
  6. If you collected subscribers on Linktree, export them from there first, since contacts don't transfer.
  7. Replace the link in your Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and other bios, and check Insights after a day to confirm clicks are being counted.

Running it yourself

LinktreeIsDead is a Next.js app that uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore in production, and local JSON files when Firebase isn't configured. Run npm ci and npm run dev, then open http://localhost:3000/linktreeisdead; a demo account is available in development. For production set the NEXT_PUBLIC_FIREBASE_* variables, FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON and AUTH_SECRET (which enables guest sessions). Images are resized in the browser before upload, and unused uploads are cleaned up after six hours. npm test and npm run typecheck run the checks.

Limitations

  • No custom domain. Your page lives at a path on the site that hosts it, which makes the URL longer than a Linktree address.
  • No import from Linktree, and one page per account with no team seats.
  • No selling, payments or shop features, by design.
  • No AI features, Instagram auto-replies or follower stats, because those depend on paid APIs or social platform access.
  • The social planner is a calendar for your own planning; it doesn't publish posts.
  • No direct sync to mailing-list tools; you export contacts as CSV.
  • No mobile app; you edit in the browser.

FAQ

Do visitors need an account to see my page?

No. Your page is an ordinary public web page that anyone with the link can open.

Can I use my own domain?

Not yet. Pages are served under the LinktreeIsDead path on whichever site hosts the app.

Can I collect email addresses?

Yes, with subscribe and contact forms on your page. Contacts appear under Audience, where you can search them and export a CSV for your mailing tool.

How are clicks counted?

Each link goes through a short redirect that records the click, filters out known bots, then sends the visitor to the address you saved.

Can I sell products from my page?

No. Selling features are left out on purpose. You can still link to your own shop or tip page like any other link.
