Free alternatives to paid SaaS

20 write-ups · Last reviewed October 26, 2026

Each page below covers one paid product and the free, open-source app on this site that replaces it. They're written to help you decide whether switching is worth it, not to sell you on it.

Every write-up follows the same outline. It explains what the paid product does and how much of that the free version covers, then puts the two side by side in a feature table with honest "partial" and "not yet" entries. It lists what the paid product costs today, with a link to its pricing page and the date it was checked, and walks through moving your data across. It also explains how to run your own copy, and lists the limitations you should know about before you rely on it.

The free apps are young open-source projects. They're good at the core job and still missing things the paid products have spent years on, such as deep integrations, admin controls and compliance features. If you need those, the paid product is probably still the right choice, and the write-up will say so. For a broader look at the trade-offs, read free alternatives to the SaaS tools most teams pay for and how to judge an open-source app before you rely on it.
