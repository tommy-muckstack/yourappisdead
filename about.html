About

YourAppIsDead is an open-source marketplace of free replacements for the software people rent every month. Each app, like CalendlyIsDead or NotionIsDead, does the core job of a paid product, costs nothing to use, and publishes its full source code under the Apache-2.0 license.

How it works

Every app is its own open-source project with its own repository. This site is the front door: it lists the apps, links straight into each one, and hosts a few games for anyone killing time between subscriptions. Web apps open right in your browser, most without an account. Desktop apps link to their download pages.

Open source, all the way down

This site is open source too. Anyone can report a bug, fix one, improve an app, or propose a new one. Start with the contributing guide or browse all the repositories on GitHub.

Keeping the lights on

The apps are free. Hosting isn't, so the hub may show ads from Google AdSense. The apps themselves stay free and open source. The privacy policy explains what that means for your data.
