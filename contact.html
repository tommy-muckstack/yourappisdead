Contact

YourAppIsDead is run in the open, so the best way to reach the people behind it is on GitHub, where every conversation is public and searchable.

Bugs, ideas and questions

Open an issue on the YourAppIsDead repository. For a problem with a specific app, open it on that app's repository instead; each app links to its repo from its tile on the home page.

Request an app

Use the request form on the home page; it opens a pre-filled GitHub issue the paid app you want replaced.

Privacy

To change your ad and cookie choices, use the "Privacy & cookie settings" or "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" links at the bottom of any page. For anything else about your data, open an issue or read the privacy policy.

Security

Please don't report security problems in a public issue. Follow the private reporting steps in our security policy.
