Privacy

Last updated October 16, 2026

YourAppIsDead is an open-source hub for free replacements of paid apps. This page covers the hub at yourappisdead.com. Each app linked from here is its own open-source project; its repository describes what it stores.

Analytics

We use PostHog to count visits and see which apps and games people use. It records pages viewed, clicks on app tiles and share buttons, game scores, and the approximate location, browser and device derived from your request. It sets a first-party cookie so repeat visits count as one visitor. We don't record your screen, we don't ask for your name or email, and analytics is skipped entirely if your browser sends Do Not Track.

Advertising

The hub may show ads served by Google AdSense to help cover hosting. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ad Settings, or opt out of many third-party ad vendors at aboutads.info. Google's use of advertising data is described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Leaderboards

If you submit a game score, we store the name you type and the score. Your IP address is held for about a minute to rate-limit submissions, then discarded. Game best scores are also kept in your own browser's local storage.

Hosting

The site runs on Vercel, which processes standard request logs (IP address, user agent) to serve and protect the site.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't fingerprint your browser.
  • We don't require an account to use the hub.

Questions

Open an issue on the GitHub repository. Since the site is open source, you can also read exactly what it does in the code.
